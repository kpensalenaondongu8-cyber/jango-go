Players simultaneously shape their hands into one of three symbols to determine a winner based on a set of rules.The Hand Signs✊ Rock: A closed fist.✋ Paper: An open, flat hand.✌️ Scissors: An extended index and middle finger forming a "V".
**The Rules (How to Win)Each sign beats one symbol and loses to another.**
#The hierarchy is cyclical:🪨 Rock beats Scissors: Rock crushes scissors.
#📄 Paper beats Rock: Paper covers rock.
#✂️ Scissors beat Paper: Scissors cut paper.
#🤝 Tie: If both players show the same sign, it is a draw, and they play again.
